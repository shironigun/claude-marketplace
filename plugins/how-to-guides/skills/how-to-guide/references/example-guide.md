## Before you begin

Customers forget when their mower, tractor or generator is due for service, and a reminder call from a busy service desk is the first thing that slips. Service Reminders finds every unit that's due from your closed repair orders, writes the text for you, and sends it once you approve it.

Service Reminders works from the start. Your store begins with a 12-month service interval and texts going out between 9 AM and 6 PM, so a service writer can send the first reminders today.

You need:

- Access to **Service** in the left menu.
- A texting number for your location. Your account already has one if you text customers from **Messages**.

> [!AVAILABILITY]
> Service Reminders is available on every plan that includes texting. To add texting to your account, contact our support team.

> [!AI]
> Service Reminders is an AI feature. It writes each reminder from the unit's last repair order, and it can get a detail wrong, such as the service that's due, so read every text before you send it. You stay in control: it never sends a text on its own.

## How it works

### Where reminders come from

Each morning, Service Reminders reads the **closed** repair orders at your location and finds the units whose last service was 12 months ago or more (or the interval you choose). Each unit becomes one reminder in the **Due** list, with the customer, the unit, the date of the last service and a text written for that customer.

It reads only closed repair orders and the units on them. It doesn't read open repair orders, parts counter sales, quotes or emails. A unit serviced somewhere else, or only ever sold to the customer, isn't in the list until it has a closed repair order with you.

### Who the customer hears from

A reminder text comes from your location's texting number and ends with your location's name, not the name of the person who sent it. Replies arrive in **Messages**, like any other text.

| On screen | What it means |

| **Due** | Units due for service that nobody has sent or skipped yet. |

The **Location** drop-down at the top-right of the screen narrows the lists. Select a column heading to sort by it.

## Send or skip a reminder

Work through the **Due** list once a day, for example first thing in the morning:

1. Select **Service** in the left menu, then the **Reminders** tab.

   You'll see the **Due** list, oldest service first.

2. Select a reminder.

   You'll see the **Reminder** drawer open on the right, with the unit's last repair order and the suggested text.

3. Read the suggested text against the repair order, and change anything that's wrong. This is what the customer reads.
4. Select **Send**.

   You'll see **Reminder sent** at the bottom of the screen, and the reminder moves to **Sent**.

> [!WARNING]
> If the customer has already booked by phone or at the counter, don't send the text. Select **Skip** instead, and choose **Already booked**.

### Skip a reminder

Select **Skip** in the drawer, then choose why: **Already booked**, **Unit sold**, **Customer asked not to be texted** or **Other**. The reminder leaves the **Due** list and doesn't come back for that service.

> [!DONE]
> The **Due** list is empty, or holds only reminders you're coming back to. Every sent reminder is on the **Sent** tab with your name and the time.

## Change when reminders go out

Your store's defaults work for most businesses. Change them only when your service plan is different:

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu, then **Service Reminders**.
2. In **Service interval**, choose how long after a closed repair order a unit is due: from **3 months** to **24 months**.
3. In **Sending hours**, choose the earliest and latest time a text can go out, in your location's time.
4. Select **Save**.

   You'll see **Settings saved**. Units that now fall due appear in the **Due** list the next morning.

## See who booked

Select the **Booked** tab. It lists every customer who booked a service within 30 days of their reminder, with the date of the reminder and the date of the booking. Use it to see which reminders bring customers back.

## Tips to work faster

- Sort the **Due** list by **Last service** and start with the oldest. Those customers are the most overdue.
- Skip with **Already booked** as soon as a customer books by phone. It keeps the list honest.

## Questions clients ask

**Does it text customers on its own?**
No. Nothing goes out until someone at your store reads the text and selects **Send**.

**Can the suggested text be wrong?**
Yes. It's written by AI from the last repair order. Check the unit and the service before you send.

**Who will the customer see it from?**
Your location's texting number, signed with your location's name.

**Will a customer get two reminders for the same unit?**
No. A unit gets one reminder for each service that comes due, whether you send it or skip it.

**What about customers who asked not to be texted?**
They're left out of the list. A customer who has opted out of texts never appears in **Due**.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No texting number for this location.** | Your location can't text yet. Contact our support team to add a number. |
| A unit you serviced last year isn't in **Due** | Its repair order is still open, or it closed less than your service interval ago. Close the repair order, or check **Service interval** in **Settings › Service Reminders**. |
| **Send** is greyed out | It's outside your **Sending hours**. Send it during those hours, or change them. |
| The suggested text names the wrong service | Change the text before you send it. |
