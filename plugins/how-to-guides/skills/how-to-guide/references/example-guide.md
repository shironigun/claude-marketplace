## Before you begin

Clients forget when an invoice is due, and a reminder call from a busy front office is the first thing that slips. Payment Reminders finds every overdue invoice, writes the text for you, and sends it once you approve it.

Payment Reminders works from the start. Your account begins with reminders 7 days after the due date and texts going out between 9 AM and 6 PM, so an office manager can send the first reminders today.

You need:

- Access to **Invoices** in the left menu.
- A texting number for your location. Your account already has one if you text clients from **Inbox**.

> [!AVAILABILITY]
> Payment Reminders is available on every plan that includes texting. To add texting to your account, contact our support team.

> [!AI]
> Payment Reminders is an AI feature. It writes each reminder from the invoice, and it can get a detail wrong, such as the amount that's due, so read every text before you send it. You stay in control: it never sends a text on its own.

## How it works

### Where reminders come from

Each morning, Payment Reminders reads the **open** invoices at your location and finds the ones that are 7 days or more past their due date (or the delay you choose). Each invoice becomes one reminder in the **Due** list, with the client, the invoice number, the amount due and a text written for that client.

It reads only open invoices that have been sent. It doesn't read draft invoices, quotes, paid invoices or emails. An invoice you haven't sent yet isn't in the list until it's sent and past due.

### Who the client hears from

A reminder text comes from your location's texting number and ends with your business name, not the name of the person who sent it. Replies arrive in **Inbox**, like any other text.

## Send or skip a reminder

Work through the **Due** list once a day, for example first thing in the morning:

1. Select **Invoices** in the left menu, then the **Reminders** tab.

   You'll see the **Due** list, oldest due date first.

2. Select a reminder.

   You'll see the **Reminder** drawer open on the right, with the invoice and the suggested text.

3. Read the suggested text against the invoice, and change anything that's wrong. This is what the client reads.
4. Select **Send**.

   You'll see **Reminder sent** at the bottom of the screen, and the reminder moves to **Sent**.

> [!WARNING]
> If the client has already paid by phone or in person, don't send the text. Select **Skip** instead, and choose **Already paid**.

### Skip a reminder

Select **Skip** in the drawer, then choose why: **Already paid**, **Payment plan agreed**, **Client asked not to be texted** or **Other**. The reminder leaves the **Due** list and doesn't come back for that invoice.

> [!DONE]
> The **Due** list is empty, or holds only reminders you're coming back to. Every sent reminder is on the **Sent** tab with your name and the time.

## Change when reminders go out

Your account's defaults work for most businesses. Change them only when your payment terms are different:

1. Select **Settings** in the left menu, then **Payment Reminders**.
2. In **Reminder delay**, choose how long after the due date an invoice is ready for a reminder: from **1 day** to **30 days**.
3. In **Sending hours**, choose the earliest and latest time a text can go out, in your location's time.
4. Select **Save**.

   You'll see **Settings saved**. Invoices that are now ready appear in the **Due** list the next morning.

## See who paid

Select the **Paid** tab. It lists every client who paid within 30 days of their reminder, with the date of the reminder and the date of the payment. Use it to see which reminders get invoices paid.

## Tips to work faster

- Sort the **Due** list by **Due date** and start with the oldest. Those invoices are the most overdue.
- Skip with **Already paid** as soon as a client pays by phone. It keeps the list honest.

## Questions office managers ask

**Does it text clients on its own?**
No. Nothing goes out until someone on your team reads the text and selects **Send**.

**Can the suggested text be wrong?**
Yes. It's written by AI from the invoice. Check the invoice number and the amount before you send.

**Who will the client see it from?**
Your location's texting number, signed with your business name.

**Will a client get two reminders for the same invoice?**
No. An invoice gets one reminder, whether you send it or skip it.

**What about clients who asked not to be texted?**
They're left out of the list. A client who has opted out of texts never appears in **Due**.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No texting number for this location.** | Your location can't text yet. Contact our support team to add a number. |
| An overdue invoice isn't in **Due** | It hasn't been sent yet, or it's fewer days past due than your reminder delay. Send the invoice, or check **Reminder delay** in **Settings › Payment Reminders**. |
| **Send** is greyed out | It's outside your **Sending hours**. Send it during those hours, or change them. |
| The suggested text shows the wrong amount | Change the text before you send it. |
