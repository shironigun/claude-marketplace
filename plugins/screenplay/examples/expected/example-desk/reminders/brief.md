# Reminders

## Purpose
A queue of follow-up reminders in three tabs (Open, Snoozed, Sent). A user opens a reminder in a drawer, then sends it with a short note or snoozes it.

## Users
Account managers and their manager, who work the queue every day.

## Payoff
A reminder sent from its drawer with a note: the toast names the customer, the reminder moves to Sent, and the notification bell counts it.
