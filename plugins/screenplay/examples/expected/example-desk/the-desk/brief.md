# The Desk

## Purpose
A small desk for following up with customers: a dashboard of counters, a reminders queue where each reminder is sent with a note or snoozed, and a customers list that tags customers with an open reminder.

## Users
Account managers and their manager.

## Payoff
One reminder sent from the queue shows up across the desk: the toast, the Sent tab and the notification bell at once, and the dashboard's Sent this week counter on its next load.
